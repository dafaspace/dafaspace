dafaspace

Daniel Fainberg

Indie developer and visual artist building small, focused apps for the things I love - cinema, music, everyday life experiences.

I come from a visual background - photography, videography, video art for theatre productions. I've spent years curiously observing how images, sound and rhythm carry human experience, emotion and feeling.

Currently studying MA Professional Media Composition at Arts University Bournemouth, where I'm learning to write music for film.

Under dafaspace I make apps that I'd want to use myself, and I really do use them every day. I'm genuinely happy when I see my apps make someone's life a little easier or more joyful. I believe in people and in meaningful human connection - so nothing makes me happier than when like-minded people find each other and that small miracle of real interaction happens.

Projects

CINEMAIL
Live
Your personal cinema companion. Track films, get AI picks, share lists with friends. Eight languages, public film pages, IMDb import. Web now, iOS & Android coming soon.
🧡 Early supporters get lifetime Pro free when Cinemail goes paid in the stores.
CIRCLE OF FIFTHS
Beta
An interactive circle of fifths. Explore keys, modes and chords, hear how they relate, and test yourself with a quiz. Free, no account, works offline.
CITRUS TIMER
Not in stores yet
A Pomodoro timer shaped like an orange, living in the macOS menu bar. The fruit assembles while you focus. Finished and in daily use, waiting on release.
HUM
Beta
Sing or hum an idea and keep it. A personal creative archive for musicians, built on its own pitch detection engine that runs in the browser.
ASTROTRIP
Beta
Precise astrological charts that run entirely on your device. No server, no account, nothing leaving your phone. Readings written to be read, not assembled on the spot.

In development

Working tools rather than products. Listed so you can see what I actually spend my days on.

SCOREWEAVER
In development
A local pipeline for scoring narrated video. It analyses the voiceover, tags the story by emotion, finds where the music should turn, and proposes cues that fit the scene. Python, running entirely on my own machine.
LOCALDECK
In development
A control panel for local AI models on a Mac, plus semantic search and automatic tagging across a sound effects library. Python and FastAPI, nothing sent to the cloud.
MY DAY
Private tool
News worth reading from sources I rate myself, a day plan that fills the free hours, and the tasks, habits and goals underneath. TypeScript, local-first.
ARCHIVIST
Private tool
A catalogue for a shelf of archive drives, plugged in one at a time, so I can find what is on a disk without mounting it.
