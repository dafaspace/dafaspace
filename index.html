Daniel Fainberg

I build small, focused apps for the things I love: cinema, music, everyday life.

More about meLess

I come from a visual background - photography, videography, video art for theatre productions. I've spent years curiously observing how images, sound and rhythm carry human experience, emotion and feeling.

For five years I've been the sound designer and music editor of a YouTube channel that has grown to 27 million subscribers.

Currently studying MA Professional Media Composition at Arts University Bournemouth, where I'm learning to write music for film.

Under dafaspace I make apps that I'd want to use myself, and I really do use them every day. I'm genuinely happy when I see my apps make someone's life a little easier or more joyful. I believe in people and in meaningful human connection - so nothing makes me happier than when like-minded people find each other and that small miracle of real interaction happens.

Questions about the apps, or an idea for working together? Write to hello@dafaspace.com. I read every message.

Projects

CINEMAIL

Live
Updated this week
Your personal cinema companion. Track films, get picks from a few words, share lists with friends. Eight languages, public film pages, IMDb import. On the web now; the iPhone app is in private testing.

UNDERPICTURE

Beta
Updated this week
Send your client one link. They hear every music option under the picture, switch tracks while the video keeps playing, and rate each one Cold, Warm or Hot. The pick lands in your inbox. For editors, composers and videographers; works on any phone, in thirteen languages.
Closed beta: to make links, ask for a tester code at hello@underpicture.com.

TUNEMAIL

Beta
Updated this week
Send a song to anyone: one link that opens it in whatever app they use. On the web now as a public beta; the iPhone app is in development.
Try it without an account: paste a song link.

CIRCLE OF FIFTHS

Live
Updated this week
An interactive circle of fifths. Explore keys, modes and chords, hear how they relate, and test yourself with a quiz. Free, no account, works offline.

CITRUS TIMER

Not released yet
Updated this week
A Pomodoro timer for the Mac, shaped like an orange: a window, or a small widget on the desktop. The fruit assembles while you focus. In daily use; a signed download is the next step.

ASTROVARIUM

Beta
Updated this week
Precise astrological charts, with positions checked against NASA JPL Horizons. On the web a natal chart and everything about it is free; transits, progressions, directions, returns, synastry and tri-wheels open for a week with an access key. Runs entirely on your device: no server, no account, nothing leaves your phone. The Android version is in private testing; the iPhone version is being prepared.

In development

Working tools rather than products. Listed so you can see what I actually spend my days on.

SCOREWEAVER

Private tool
Updated this week
A local pipeline for my scoring work on narrated video. Today it turns the script and the voiceover into checked subtitles; matching music to the emotion of the story is still in research. Python, running entirely on my own machine.

LOCALDECK

Private tool
Updated this week
Semantic search and automatic tagging for a sound effects library: over 4,500 files tagged so far, the audio itself untouched. Plus a control panel for local AI models on a Mac. Python and FastAPI, nothing sent to the cloud.

MY DAY

Private tool
Updated this week
Where my working hours actually go: hours per minute of finished video, per project, against a weekly norm, filled in by Citrus Timer on its own. Also a news reader and a day plan. TypeScript, local-first.

ARCHIVIST

Private tool
Updated this week
